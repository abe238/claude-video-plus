
 Good morning everyone and welcome to the quarterly engineering review. Today we will cover three topics, the migration of our payment pipeline, the reliability numbers from the last 90 days, and the roadmap for the coming quarter. Let us begin with the migration. The payment pipeline moved from a monolithic scheduler to a queue-based architecture in early March. During the cutover we processed roughly 400,000 transactions per day with zero data loss and a median latency of 120 milliseconds. So this is a seven times improvement at the median. Second, reliability. We logged four incidents in the quarter. Two were caused by an expired certificate on the internal gateway, one by a misconfigured feature flag, and one by a disk failure in the analytics cluster. Total downtime was 43 minutes against a budget of two hours, which keeps us well inside the service level objective of 99.95%. The certificate incidents are the most embarrassing, because they were entirely preventable. We have added automated rotation and a canary check that alerts seven days before expiry. Third, the roadmap. Next quarter we plan to ship the new ingestion service, retire the legacy reporting stack, and complete the encryption project for data at rest. The ingestion service is the riskiest item because it touches every downstream consumer. We will run it in shadow mode for at least three weeks before any traffic shifts. Finally a note on hiring.
